Privacy Policy

Yeh policy batati hai ki BiharFast par aane ya hamara Idea & Feature Request Box use karne par aapki jaankari kaise handle hoti hai.

Idea form aur Web3Forms

Jab aap idea form submit karte hain, to aapka chuna hua category, idea/problem aur aapke dwara di gayi optional district aur contact details form processing ke liye Web3Forms ko bheji jaati hain. Yeh submission Web3Forms ki services ke madhyam se BiharFast tak pahunchti hai aur sujhav samajhne, jawab dene ya platform behtar banane ke liye use ho sakti hai. Form mein sirf wahi contact detail dein jise aap share karna chahte hain.

WhatsApp

WhatsApp button aapke device/browser mein WhatsApp link kholta hai aur aapke likhe category, district (agar diya ho) aur idea ke saath message tayyar karta hai. Message aapki marzi se WhatsApp par bheja jaata hai; WhatsApp ki apni privacy policy aur terms lagu hoti hain.

Cookies aur third-party advertising

Bhavishya mein BiharFast par third-party advertising, jaise Google AdSense, dikhayi ja sakti hai. Ad partners cookies ya milti-julti technologies ka istemal kar sakte hain taaki ads dikhayein, unki frequency manage karein ya ads ki performance samjhein. Google ke advertising cookies aur unhe manage karne ke tareeqon ke liye Google ki Privacy & Terms jaankari dekhein. Browser settings se cookies control ki ja sakti hain.

Analytics

Agar website analytics tools enable kiye jaate hain, to ve website ke istemal se judi technical ya usage jaankari collect kar sakte hain, jaise dekhe gaye pages aur device/browser details. Aise tools aur data ka istemal unke apne privacy terms ke adheen hoga. Is samay kisi analytics provider ya data retention period ka vaada nahi kiya ja raha.

Data ka istemal aur suraksha

Hum prapt jaankari ko sujhav dekhne, zaroorat par sampark karne aur website sudharne ke liye istemal karte hain. Internet par data bhejne ya store karne ka koi bhi tareeqa poori suraksha ki guarantee nahi de sakta. Apni submission mein password, financial details ya anya sensitive personal information na bhejein.

Aapke vikalp aur sampark

Aap optional contact details na dene ka chunav kar sakte hain. Apne bheje gaye data ke baare mein sawaal ya request ke liye devanuragai@gmail.com par sampark karein.

Policy mein badlav

Website ya applicable requirements badalne par is policy ko update kiya ja sakta hai. Nayi policy isi page par publish hogi.
