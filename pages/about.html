Kaam ki cheezein.
Sabke liye.

BiharFast ek independent digital initiative hai. Hamara maksad Bihar ke logon ki rozmarra ki zarooraton ko samajhkar free, simple aur practical digital experiences ki buniyad rakhna hai.

Yeh shuruaat kyun?

Internet par bahut saari jaankari hai, lekin har baar woh digital service ya experience nahi milta jo kisi real-life problem ko aasaan kare. BiharFast par hum copy-paste content ke bajay logon ke asli sujhav sunna chahte hain — padhai, kaam, paisa bachane aur daily life se judi zarooraton ke baare mein.

Logon ke sujhav se

Yeh abhi ek nayi shuruaat hai. Filhaal hum Bihar ke logon ki priorities aur ideas samajh rahe hain; koi naya utility launch hone ka daawa nahi karte. Aap apni problem ya suggestion Idea Box mein share kar sakte hain, taaki aage ka platform real zarooraton ke hisaab se ban sake.

Kaun bana raha hai?

BiharFast ko Anurag Kumar ne shuru kiya hai. Yeh ek private, independent platform hai — kisi sarkari sanstha ka hissa nahi.
