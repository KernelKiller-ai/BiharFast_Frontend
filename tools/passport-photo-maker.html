Guide 01

Sarkari Form ke liye Photo & Signature KB Resize kaise karein?

BPSC, CSBC aur SSC online application bharte waqt sabse jyada pareshani photo aur signature ke file size (KB) ko lekar aati hai. Mobile camera se li gayi photo 3MB se 6MB tak hoti hai, jise form reject kar deta hai.

  1. Photo Upload Karein: 'Dropzone' par tap karke apne phone se photo ya signature chunein.
  2. Preset Select Karein: Control panel me BPSC Photo ya CSBC chip dabayein. Target size automatically 50 KB set ho jayega.
  3. Check Size: Niche preview bar me dekhein ki output size 50 KB se kam ho gaya hai.
  4. Download Karein: 'Download File' button par click karke instant JPG download karein.
Guide 02

UTI / NSDL PAN Card ke liye 213×213 Pixel Photo kaise banayein?

PAN Card online form (UTIITSL aur NSDL) par regular photo upload karne par 'Invalid Dimension' ka error aata hai, kyunki wahan strict 213x213 pixels aur 300 DPI ki shart hoti hai.

  1. Photo upload karke 'KB Resizer' tab me jayein.
  2. PAN Photo (213×213) chip par click karein. Tool automatically width 213px aur height 213px lock kar deta hai.
  3. File size automatically 30 KB se kam adjust ho jayegi.
  4. Signature ke liye 'PAN Sign (400×200)' select karein.
Guide 03

Bihar RTPS (serviceonline.bihar.gov.in) ke liye PDF 200KB kaise banayein?

Bihar me Jati Praman Patra (Caste), Aawasiya (Residence), Aay (Income) ya Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) certificate banwate waqt Aadhaar card aur dastavez 200 KB se kam PDF me maange jaate hain.

  1. Apne Aadhaar card ya document ki photo Studio me upload karein.
  2. Tab bar me 'PDF 200KB' tab par click karein.
  3. 'RTPS Max 200 KB' preset chunein. Yeh tool image ko bina padhne laayak text khoye perfect 195 KB PDF me convert karta hai.
  4. 'Generate RTPS PDF' button dabayein aur turant PDF download karein.
Guide 04

Photo par Candidate ka Name aur Date (DOP) kaise likhein?

Bihar Police (CSBC) aur SSC me yeh niyam hai ki photo par candidate ka naam aur kis din photo li gayi (Date of Photo - DOP) bottom strip par saaf likha hona chahiye.

  1. Photo upload karke 'Name & Date' tab me jayein.
  2. Apna pura naam (jaise: AMIT KUMAR) likhein.
  3. Date of Photo me aaj ki ya recent tareekh darj karein (jaise: DOP: 15/11/2026).
  4. Tool photo ke niche safed patti (white strip) par bold black font me details print kar dega.

Aksar Puche Jaane Wale Sawal (FAQ)

Photo resizer, signature compress aur RTPS PDF tool ke bare me zaroori sawal-jawab.

Photo resize karne par kya photo ki quality kharab ho jati hai?

Nahi! BiharFast Studio ek multi-step adaptive canvas algorithm ka upyog karta hai jo image ke resolution aur compression quality ko balance karta hai. Isse photo ka chehra aur signature ke baareek akshar bilkul clear rehte hain aur file size 20KB-50KB me convert ho jati hai.

Mobile phone se li gayi signature ki photo ko saaf (B&W) kaise karein?

Mobile se photo kheenchne par kagaz par parchhayi (shadow) ya peela-pan aa jata hai. BiharFast Studio ke 'Touch-Up' tab me jakar 'Clean Signature (B&W)' button par click karein. Yeh background ko ekdum safed kar dega aur signature ki siyahi (ink) ko gehra black bana dega jisse portal par signature turant accept ho sake.

Bihar RTPS portal par 'File size exceeds 200 KB' ka error kyun aata hai?

Bihar RTPS (serviceonline.bihar.gov.in) ka server 200 KB se badi file ko reject kar deta hai. Hamare Studio ke 'PDF 200KB' tab me 'RTPS Max 200 KB' option chunein. Yeh tool document ko strictly 180 KB se 198 KB ke beech lock karta hai jisse error kabhi nahi aayega.

Kya yeh tool bilkul muft (free) hai aur isme koi watermark aayega?

Haan, BiharFast Studio 100% free hai. Isme kisi bhi tarah ka watermark, branding ya hidden charge nahi hai. Cyber cafe operators aur students jitni chahein utni photos aur documents resize kar sakte hain.

Kya photo par Date of Photo (DOP) likhna sabhi exam me anivarya hai?

Nahi, har exam me anivarya nahi hota. CSBC Bihar Police, SSC aur kuch vishesh BPSC vigyapanon me candidate ka naam aur DOP likhna mandatory hota hai. Notification padh kar agar zaroorat ho toh hamare 'Name & Date' tab ka upyog karein, anyatha saadharan passport photo download karein.

Kya bina internet ke bhi yeh tool kaam karta hai?

Ek baar page load ho jane ke baad image resizing aur photo touching poori tarah aapke device ke browser me hoti hai. Isliye agar internet thodi der ke liye disconnect bhi ho jaye, tab bhi aap photo resize aur download kar sakte hain.
